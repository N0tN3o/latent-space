📘 Operator Manual & Troubleshooting

  1. Power on the WiFi Router first.
  2. Power on Jetson B (The Brain) and wait 30 seconds.
  3. Power on Jetson A (The Eye) and RPi 4 (The Hand).
  4. The system will auto-connect within 60 seconds.
  5. Verify status in the Monitor tab of this dashboard.

  • Printer Jammed: Check the physical printer attached to RPi4. Clear the queue in the 'Monitor' tab if needed.
  • No Gaze Detected: Ensure Jetson A camera lens is uncovered and lighting is sufficient.
  • Network Error (Red Dot): Use the 'Monitor' tab to restart the specific service that is down.
  • Web Page Not Loading: Ensure you are connected to the correct WiFi network.

The system performs a daily backup automatically at 04:00 AM.

To clear disk space manually, go to the Prints tab and click "Prune Old Files".
